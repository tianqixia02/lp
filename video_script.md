## 📜 Narration Script (≈ 90 seconds)

**[SCENE 1 — HOOK | 0:00–0:10]**
*Voiceover (V.O.):*
"Every year, the equivalent of every single car on Earth — stacked in shipping containers — passes through ONE port. This is the Port of Shanghai… and it never, ever sleeps."

---

**[SCENE 2 — SCALE | 0:10–0:25]**
*V.O.:*
"Stretching over 3,600 kilometers of shoreline along China's coast, Shanghai's port handles more than 850 million tons of cargo every single year. That's roughly 47 million containers — if you lined them up end-to-end, they would circle the entire planet… three times."

---

**[SCENE 3 — HISTORY | 0:25–0:38]**
*V.O.:*
"Shanghai has been a trading hub for over 700 years. But it was the opening of the Yangshan Deep-Water Terminal in 2005 that launched it to the top of the world rankings — a man-made island connected to the mainland by the world's longest sea bridge: 32 kilometers of concrete stretching over the open East China Sea."

---

**[SCENE 4 — HOW IT WORKS | 0:38–1:00]**
*V.O.:*
"Inside, it's a city unto itself. Giant cranes — some taller than the Statue of Liberty — lift containers off ships in seconds. But here's what's wild: most of those cranes are operated by *nobody*. Yangshan's fourth phase is the world's largest automated terminal, run entirely by robots and AI. Autonomous trucks, laser-guided cranes, and a control room that looks straight out of a sci-fi film manage everything 24 hours a day."

---

**[SCENE 5 — WHY IT MATTERS | 1:00–1:15]**
*V.O.:*
"Why does this matter to you? Because roughly 90% of everything you own has spent time on a container ship. Your phone, your sneakers, your coffee — the odds are good that they passed through Shanghai first. One disruption here can ripple through global supply chains for *months*."

---

**[SCENE 6 — OUTRO / CALL TO ACTION | 1:15–1:30]**
*V.O.:*
"Shanghai's Port isn't just the world's busiest harbor — it's the heartbeat of global trade. And it's only getting bigger. If you found that fascinating, subscribe — because next time, we're going inside the most automated factory on Earth."
