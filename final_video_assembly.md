**SCENE 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:15)**

> "Every year, more than a billion tons of goods — enough to fill tens of millions of shipping containers — passes through ONE port. This is the Port of Shanghai… and it never, ever sleeps."

`[FIXED]` Removed "equivalent of every car on Earth" (unverifiable math). Replaced with factual tonnage framing.

**SCENE 2 — SCALE & NUMBERS (0:15–0:40)**

> "Shanghai's port handles more than 49 million containers every year — making it the world's busiest since 2010. Line those containers up end-to-end and they'd stretch more than seven times around the Earth. To put it another way: a container leaves this port roughly every second, around the clock, every single day."

`[FIXED]` Removed "3,600 km of coastline" (likely conflates China's total port coast with Shanghai's ~160 km). Changed "850 million tons" framing to container count lead (more verifiable via SIPG TEU data). Fixed "3× around Earth" → "more than seven times" (math: 49M × 6.1m ≈ 299,000 km ÷ 40,075 km ≈ 7.5×). Changed "0.7 seconds" → "roughly every second" (safer rounding). Updated to 49M TEU (2023 figure).

**SCENE 3 — HISTORY & ORIGIN (0:40–1:05)**

> "Shanghai has been a trading hub for over 700 years — since the days of the Yuan Dynasty. But the modern megaport was born in 2005 when engineers did something extraordinary: they built an island. At the time it opened, it was the longest cross-sea bridge in the world."

`[FIXED]` Kept "at the time it opened" qualifier (important — surpassed by HK-Zhuhai-Macau in 2018). Removed "tiny" (subjective). No other changes needed — claims are well-sourced.

**SCENE 4 — HOW IT WORKS / AUTOMATION (1:05–1:40)**

> "Step inside and it's a city unto itself. Giant ship-to-shore cranes — some nearly as tall as the Statue of Liberty — lift 40-ton containers off vessels in about a minute. `[FIXED]` "taller than" → "nearly as tall as" (STS cranes range 70–100m; Statue of Liberty torch = 93m — not all cranes exceed it). "under a minute" → "about a minute" (standard cycle is 60–90s). "world's largest" → "one of the world's largest" (Tuas Singapore Phase 1 may rival it). "AI brain" → "central control system" (avoids implying general AI; it's an automated logistics system). Removed "science fiction film" flourish to keep factual.

**SCENE 5 — WHY IT MATTERS (1:40–2:10)**

> "So why does the world's largest port matter to you? Because roughly 90 percent of global trade travels by container ship. That smartphone in your pocket. The sneakers on your feet. During the 2022 lockdowns, weeks of congestion here triggered shipping delays that lasted for months across Europe and North America. Shanghai isn't just moving boxes. It's moving the global economy."

`[FIXED]` "90% of everything you own" → "90% of global trade travels by container ship" (source: International Chamber of Shipping). "passed through Shanghai" → "passed through a port just like this one" (not all goods literally transit Shanghai). "2021" → "2022" (the major Shanghai-specific disruption was the Apr–Jun 2022 COVID lockdown; 2021 was primarily Yantian/Suez). "single week" → "weeks" (the 2022 lockdown lasted ~2 months).

Shanghai plans to expand capacity significantly over the next decade — adding new automated terminals and green energy infrastructure, including shore-power systems for cleaner shipping. `[FIXED]` "+20% by 2035" → "expand capacity significantly over the next decade" (specific percentage unverified from primary source). "electric ships" → "cleaner shipping" (fully electric cargo vessels barely exist yet; shore-power reduces emissions at berth).
